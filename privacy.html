Privacy notice

Last updated 13 October 2026

This notice explains how the ConfidentIQ website at www.confidentiq.com handles information about its visitors. The short version: it handles almost none.

What this site collects

This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no third-party trackers and does not ask you to create an account or fill in a form. We do not collect names, email addresses or any other personal information through the site itself. Your choice of light or dark theme is kept in your own browser and never sent to us.

Hosting

The site is served by a hosting provider that, like any web server, may keep short-lived technical logs containing your IP address and the pages you requested in order to operate and secure the service. We do not use those logs to identify or profile visitors.

Fonts

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches the font files, Google receives the technical request details needed to serve them. Google describes how it handles those requests in its own privacy policy.

If you contact us

If you email us at hello@confidentiq.com, we will hold your message and contact details for as long as needed to answer you and follow up on what you asked. We will not add you to a mailing list or share your details with anyone else without asking first.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to the address above and we will respond promptly.

Changes

If we change how this site works, for example by adding a contact form or analytics, we will update this notice and the date at the top before the change goes live.
