What we do

One picture. Every identity. Someone accountable.

Modern organisations run on far more non‑human identities than human ones: the services, workloads, pipelines and agents that hold credentials and keys to act on their behalf. Most teams cannot say with confidence what all of them are, what depends on them, or who is responsible when one expires, leaks or is left behind. We make that knowable, and keep it that way.

  • See everything

    One trusted picture of the identities, credentials and keys across every system you run, kept current as they change.

  • Know who answers

    Each one tied to the application it serves and the person accountable for it, so nothing is left without an owner.

  • Act before it breaks

    Expiry, exposure and abandoned access surfaced early, ranked by what each one would take down with it.

  • Fix through your own tools

    Problems are resolved through the systems your teams already use, with a record of every change.

How it works

From first connection to lasting confidence.

Nothing to install in the path of your traffic, and no new system to babysit. ConfidentIQ reads from the tools you already run and gives you back one coherent view.

  1. 1

    Connect

    Read-only connections to the systems that already own your identities, certificates and keys. Nothing changes on day one.

  2. 2

    Understand

    Everything joined into one picture: what exists, what depends on it, who owns it, and what would break if it failed.

  3. 3

    Act

    Fix what matters through the tools you already trust, with approval where you want it and evidence at every step.

Principles

Built to be trusted by the teams that trust nothing.

Security teams are right to be careful about who they let near their keys. Three commitments shape everything we build.

  • We never hold your keys.

    Your secrets stay in the systems built to keep them. We work with references and evidence, never the material itself.

  • We never sit in the path of a login.

    If ConfidentIQ went offline, nothing in your estate would stop working. We inform and orchestrate; we do not gate.

  • We never replace the tools you already trust.

    Every fix is routed through the system that owns the asset, so your existing controls, approvals and audit trails stay intact.
